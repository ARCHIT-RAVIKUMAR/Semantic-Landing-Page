Build something in 48 hours.

CodeSprint Weekend is a two-day build event for students. Pick a track Friday night, build with a team, and demo what you made Sunday evening.

Reserve a seat

What this weekend actually looks like

Teams of two to four pick one track on Friday evening, then spend Saturday and Sunday building. There's no set curriculum — mentors are around to unblock you, not to lecture. Sunday closes with five-minute demos in front of everyone.

Who it's for

Any student comfortable with at least one programming language. No prior hackathon experience needed.

What you'll need

Your own laptop and charger. We handle the rest — wifi, power strips, food, and mentor time.

What you leave with

A working project, a GitHub repo you can point to, and whatever your team decides to keep building after.
